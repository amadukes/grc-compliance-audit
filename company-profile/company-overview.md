# River Valley Medical Services (RVMS)

# Company Overview

River Valley Medical Services is a fictional healthcare services organization
with approximately 75 employees. The organization uses technology to support
daily business operations and stores sensitive employee and patient information.

# Technology Environment

RVMS currently uses:

- Windows laptops and desktop computers
- Microsoft 365 for email and productivity
- Cloud-based file storage
- Internal business network
- Wireless network
- Remote access for employees
- Employee email accounts
- Electronic patient and business records

# IT Department

The IT department consists of three employees responsible for:

- User account management
- Network administration
- Endpoint security
- Software updates
- Technical support
- Data backup
- Security monitoring

# Sensitive Information

RVMS handles several types of sensitive information:

- Patient information
- Employee personal information
- Login credentials
- Financial information
- Business records

# Security Concerns

Management has requested a cybersecurity risk and compliance assessment
because the organization has not recently performed a formal security review.

The assessment will identify important assets, cybersecurity risks,
security control gaps, and recommended improvements.
