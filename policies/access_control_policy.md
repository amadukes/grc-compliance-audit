# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines requirements for controlling access to RVMS information
systems and sensitive information.

## Requirements

- Each user must have an individual account.
- Shared accounts should be avoided unless specifically authorized.
- Multi-factor authentication should be required for critical systems.
- Access must be based on job responsibilities and business need.
- Administrative privileges must be limited to authorized personnel.
- User access must be reviewed periodically.
- Accounts must be disabled when an employee leaves the organization.
- Access should be modified when an employee changes roles.

## Principle of Least Privilege
Users should receive only the minimum level of access required to perform
their job responsibilities.

## Access Reviews
IT and management should periodically review accounts and permissions to
identify unnecessary or inappropriate access.
