# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes acceptable use requirements for River Valley Medical
Services (RVMS) technology resources.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and authorized users who
access RVMS systems, networks, devices, or data.

## Policy Requirements
Users must:

- Use company technology primarily for authorized business purposes.
- Protect usernames, passwords, and authentication information.
- Avoid accessing suspicious or unauthorized websites.
- Never install unauthorized software.
- Never disable security software or security controls.
- Protect sensitive patient and employee information.
- Report suspicious emails, security incidents, or lost devices to IT.
- Follow all organizational security policies.

## Prohibited Activities
Users may not:

- Share account credentials.
- Attempt unauthorized access to systems or information.
- Download malicious or unapproved software.
- Use company systems for illegal activities.
- Circumvent security controls.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in loss of system access or other disciplinary action
in accordance with organizational procedures.
