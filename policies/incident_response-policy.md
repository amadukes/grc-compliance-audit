# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes a process for responding to cybersecurity incidents
at River Valley Medical Services.

## Examples of Security Incidents

- Phishing
- Malware
- Ransomware
- Unauthorized account access
- Lost or stolen devices
- Data exposure
- Suspicious network activity

## Incident Response Process

### 1. Preparation
Maintain security tools, backups, contact information, and response procedures.

### 2. Detection and Analysis
Identify suspicious activity and determine the scope and severity of the
incident.

### 3. Containment
Limit the spread or impact of the incident by isolating affected accounts,
devices, or systems when appropriate.

### 4. Eradication
Remove malicious software, compromised accounts, or other causes of the
incident.

### 5. Recovery
Restore affected systems and verify that normal operations can safely resume.

### 6. Lessons Learned
Document the incident, identify improvements, and update security controls
or procedures when necessary.

## Reporting
Employees must immediately report suspected cybersecurity incidents to the
IT department.
