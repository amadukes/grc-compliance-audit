# Executive Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Summary

## Organization
River Valley Medical Services (RVMS)

## Assessment Objective
The purpose of this assessment was to identify cybersecurity risks,
evaluate existing security practices, and recommend controls to improve
the organization's security posture.

## Assessment Scope
The assessment included:

- Employee endpoints
- User accounts
- Microsoft 365
- Corporate email
- Internal and wireless networks
- Patient and employee information
- Cloud storage
- Backup systems

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several areas requiring improvement.

### Credential Security
Weak or reused passwords and inconsistent multi-factor authentication
increase the risk of unauthorized account access.

### Phishing
Employees may receive malicious emails designed to steal credentials
or deliver malware.

### Access Control
Excessive permissions could allow users to access information that is
not required for their job responsibilities.

### Security Monitoring
Limited centralized monitoring could delay the detection of suspicious
activity.

### Incident Response
The organization does not currently have a formal incident response
process.

### Backup and Recovery
Backups are available but restoration procedures are not regularly tested.

## Priority Recommendations

1. Require multi-factor authentication for critical systems.
2. Implement role-based access control and least privilege.
3. Conduct recurring security awareness and phishing training.
4. Improve centralized security logging and monitoring.
5. Establish and test an incident response plan.
6. Maintain isolated backups and regularly test restoration procedures.

## Conclusion
RVMS can reduce its cybersecurity risk by prioritizing identity security,
employee awareness, monitoring, incident response, and recovery
capabilities.

This assessment should be reviewed periodically as organizational
technology, threats, and business requirements change.
